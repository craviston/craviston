    Terms & Conditions
    1. Contest period. The contest runs from 21 October 2026 until 11:59 PM IST on 7 November 2026. The result is announced on 8 November 2026.
    2. Eligibility. Open to players who are within 10 km of the Craviston outlet in Dombivli when they play. Location is checked through your device. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to take part.
    3. One entry per person. One Google account counts as one entry. Using several accounts, bots, scripts, edited game files or any other unfair method leads to disqualification.
    4. Winner. The player with the highest score at the close of the contest wins. If scores are tied, the player who reached that score first wins. Craviston's decision is final.
    5. Prize. One (1) waffle of the winner's choice from the Craviston menu. There is one winner. The prize has no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged.
    6. How the prize works. The winner first places an order for the chosen waffle on Zomato (Craviston). The price of that waffle is then refunded to the winner by Craviston. Refund method and timing are confirmed when the winner is contacted.
    7. Other charges. Shipping and delivery charges are paid by the customer. Taxes, platform fees and packaging charges, if any, are also payable by the customer. Only the waffle's item price is refunded.
    8. Address check. The delivery address of the winning order must be within 10 km of the outlet.
    9. Contacting the winner. The winner is contacted on the email linked to their Google account. If the winner does not respond within 3 days, the prize may go to the next eligible player.
    10. Redeem by. The offer must be redeemed by 30 November 2026. After this date it lapses.
    11. Your data. We use your Google name and email only to run this contest and contact the winner. The public leaderboard shows your first name and last initial. Your location is used on your device to check the 10 km zone and your exact position is not stored.
    12. Changes. Craviston may change, pause or cancel the contest or these terms if needed, for example to stop misuse. This contest is not sponsored or endorsed by Google, Zomato, Instagram or Facebook.
